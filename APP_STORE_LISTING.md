## Description

Playbook helps football coaches save play clips from X, TikTok, Instagram, and Facebook into a categorized digital playbook.

Share a post → see Saved to Playbook ✓ → reopen the app to watch muted autoplay and file it under Pass, Run, RPO, or Trick Plays. No play tap. No leaving the app for normal public posts.

Built for coaches who live in the film.
