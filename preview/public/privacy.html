Privacy Policy

PlaySave by NOSHOT, LLC · Effective October 29, 2026

This policy explains what information the PlaySave app ("PlaySave", "we", "us") collects, how we use it, and the choices you have. PlaySave is operated by NOSHOT, LLC.

Information we collect

  • Account information. You sign in with Apple. We receive a unique Apple user identifier and the email address Apple shares with us (which may be a private relay address), and your name if you choose to share it. We do not receive your Apple ID password.
  • Saved links. When you share a post to PlaySave, we store the post's web link (for example an X, Instagram, TikTok or Facebook URL), the platform, a title, and, where the platform provides it, an official embed link or thumbnail link. We do not download or store copies of the videos.
  • Categories. The play categories and sub-categories you create or choose, and which saved links belong to them.
  • Purchases. Subscriptions are bought and billed through Apple. We do not receive your payment card details. The app checks with Apple whether your subscription is active.
  • On-device preferences. Settings such as theme, likes and usage streaks are stored on your device.

We do not use third-party advertising, ad tracking, or cross-app tracking, and we do not sell your data.

Shared feed

Clips you save are added to PlaySave's shared feed so other coaches can discover them. The feed shows the post link, platform, title and category. It does not show your name, email or account details.

How we use information

  • To create and secure your account and keep you signed in.
  • To save, organize and sync your playbook across your devices.
  • To show the shared feed and play clips from the original platform.
  • To unlock paid features when your Apple subscription is active.
  • To provide support and keep the service working and safe.

Service providers and third parties

We use Supabase to host our database and authentication, and Vercel to host the web app that powers PlaySave. When a clip plays, it is loaded from the original platform (X, Instagram, TikTok or Facebook), which may collect information under its own privacy policy. Apple processes sign-in and payments under Apple's privacy policy.

Retention and deletion

We keep your account and saved links until you delete them. You can delete your account at any time in the app under Profile › Delete Account. This permanently removes your account, your saved links and your categories. You can also email us to request deletion.

Children

PlaySave is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, export or delete your information. Contact us and we will respond within a reasonable time.

Changes

We may update this policy. If we make material changes, we will update the date above and, where appropriate, notify you in the app.
