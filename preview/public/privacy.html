Privacy Policy

Playbook (Play Save) · Last updated October 15, 2026

Playbook helps coaches save and organize play clips from social platforms (X/Twitter, TikTok, Instagram, Facebook). This policy explains what we collect and how we use it.

Information we collect

  • Account data — email address and authentication identifiers (including Sign in with Apple when enabled).
  • Saved plays — source URLs you share or import, categories you assign, and related metadata (titles, thumbnails, official embed URLs).
  • Usage signals — optional on-device preferences such as theme, streaks, and personalization signals stored locally on your device.
  • Purchase status — if you subscribe via Apple In-App Purchase, entitlement status may be shared with our servers to unlock features.

How we use information

  • Provide and sync your playbook across sessions.
  • Resolve official embed / temporary playback helpers so clips can play muted in-app.
  • Authenticate you and protect account access.
  • Respond to support requests you send us.

What we do not do

  • We do not download or permanently rehost third-party social video files into our storage for App Store Guideline 5.2.3 compliance. Social playback uses official embeds or temporary streams.
  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not use your playbook content for advertising.

Third-party services

  • Supabase — authentication and database hosting for account and play data.
  • Apple — Sign in with Apple and In-App Purchases when used.
  • Social platforms — when you open or embed a source post, those platforms’ own policies apply.

Data retention & deletion

You can delete your account in Profile → Delete Account. This permanently removes your account and associated playbook data from our servers. Local device cache may remain until you uninstall the app.

Children

Playbook is intended for coaches and adults. It is not directed to children under 13.
